## Important boundaries

Response shape errors retain actual PayPal debug-ID/status metadata through `requestValidated`, with no raw body or validation exception attached. A POST parse failure has `outcomeUnknown: true` and is not automatically replayed; read-only GET shape failures are not marked as mutations. No unimplemented PayPalClient method is represented by a throwing placeholder.

`CaptureResult.raw` preserves the transient vendor response, while its typed shipping address follows the original field list and omits address_line_2. Q3 must settle the complete address normalization/hash and retained-field allowlist before persistence or real fixtures. No personal payload has been written to the database by this work.
